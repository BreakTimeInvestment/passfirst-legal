Delete your PassFirst account

You can ask us to delete your PassFirst account and the personal data linked to it at any time. Follow the steps below.

App: PassFirst (com.passfirst.app) · Developer: BreakTimeInvestment

  1. Send us an email

    Write to passfirstuktest@gmail.com from the address you use to sign in to PassFirst.

  2. Use the subject “Delete my PassFirst account”

    This helps us find your request quickly. Include the sign-in email address of the account you want deleted.

  3. We confirm and delete

    We will confirm your request by email, then delete your account and linked data within 30 days.

What happens to your data

DataWhat we do
Account details (email address and sign-in credentials)Permanently deleted
Your progress, quiz results and saved settingsPermanently deleted
Records we must keep by law, or for security and fraud preventionKept only as long as required, then deleted
Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify youMay be kept

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your progress cannot be recovered.

Using PassFirst as a guest

If you use the app as a guest, your progress is linked to an anonymous ID rather than an email address. To remove it, email us at the address above and tell us you used the app as a guest, or uninstall the app to clear the data stored on your device.

Questions

If you have any questions about your data or this process, contact passfirstuktest@gmail.com.
